# Calvin Religious Modeling

A collection of religious models developed by Calvin University students and faculty.

---

In this website you will find a variety of projects and works involving the development of applications and models related to religious behavior, social dynamics, and cultural evolution. These models are created using agent-based modeling techniques and are intended to explore and understand the complex interactions within religious communities.

Feel free to explore the different sections of the website to learn more about the models, their underlying theories, and the insights they provide into religious phenomena. Whether you are a student, researcher, or simply curious about the intersection of religion and modeling, we hope you find this resource informative and engaging.
